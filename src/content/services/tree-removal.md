## Common Reasons to Have a Tree Removed

Have you ever looked at a tree on your property and wondered if it was nearing its expiration date? Well, how exactly do you know if you should have a tree removed? Here are some common reasons Big Foot Tree Service is called upon to perform tree removal services in the Wayne, NJ area:

- The tree is rapidly decaying
- The tree poses a risk to your property (for example, if it falls it could harm your roof)
- The tree is leaning in a dangerous direction
- The tree has grown too large for your property
- The tree looks unsightly
- The tree drops harmful deposits on your lawn
- And more!
