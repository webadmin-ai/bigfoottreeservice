## The Importance of Removing Tree Stumps

If you're debating whether or not you should have a tree stump removed from your property, here's why you should go through with it:

- Tree stumps can be a safety hazard
- Tree stumps are unappealing
- Tree stumps attract insects
- Tree stumps can send fungus and disease to other nearby trees and plants
